# Nile Valley University (NVU): Student Services

## Library
The NVU library is in building L. It is open Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 to 20:00. Students borrow up to 5 books for 2 weeks with their student ID card.

## Clinic
The student clinic is on the ground floor of building C. It is open from 9:00 to 17:00. Call extension 2201 for an appointment.

## Student ID cards
A lost student ID card is replaced at the Student Affairs office, room S104. The replacement fee is 150 EGP.

## Buses
NVU buses run on lines 1 to 6 from Dokki and Maadi. A monthly bus pass costs 900 EGP and is bought at the Student Affairs office.
