# Nile Valley University (NVU): Admissions

## Application deadline
Applications for the fall semester at Nile Valley University close on 15 July. Late applications are not accepted.

## Required documents
To apply you need: your secondary school certificate, a copy of your national ID, and 4 personal photos.
The minimum secondary school score for admission is 75%.

## Application fee
The application fee is 750 EGP. It is paid online when you submit the application and is not refunded.

## Tuition fees
Tuition at NVU is 62,000 EGP per semester for all undergraduate programmes.

## Paying in installments
Tuition can be paid in 3 installments during the semester. The first installment is due before classes start.
Installments are not available for the summer semester.

## Withdrawal and refunds
A student who withdraws in the first two weeks gets 75% of the tuition back. After week 4 there is no refund.

## Contact
The Admissions Office is in building A, room A101, open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 to 15:00.
