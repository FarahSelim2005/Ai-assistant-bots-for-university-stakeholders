# Red Sea University (RSU): Admissions

## Application deadline
Applications for the fall semester at Red Sea University close on 1 June.

## Required documents
To apply you need: your secondary school certificate, a copy of your national ID, a medical certificate and 2 personal photos.
The minimum secondary school score for admission is 80%.

## Application fee
The application fee is 600 EGP.

## Tuition fees
Tuition at RSU is 55,000 EGP per semester.

## Paying in installments
Tuition can be paid in 4 installments, one each month of the semester.

## Contact
The Admissions Office is in the main building, room G05, open Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 to 14:00.
