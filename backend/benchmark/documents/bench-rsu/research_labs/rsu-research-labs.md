# Red Sea University (RSU): Research Labs

## Marine Biology Lab
The Marine Biology Lab is in building M, room M210. Its supervisor is Dr. Laila Mansour.

## Oceanography Lab
The Oceanography Lab is in room M118. It is open from 8:00 to 18:00 on working days.

## Coral Reef Field Station
The Coral Reef Field Station is in Hurghada. Book a visit at least 2 weeks ahead through the lab portal.

## Lab safety training
Lab safety training is required before using any lab. It takes 3 hours and is held every September.

## Research vessel
The research vessel Al Morgan can only be booked by staff.
