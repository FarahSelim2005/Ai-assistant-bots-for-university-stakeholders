# Delta Institute of Technology (DIT): Admissions

## Application deadline
Applications for the fall semester at the Delta Institute of Technology close on 31 August.

## Required documents
To apply you need: your secondary school certificate, a copy of your national ID, your birth certificate and 6 personal photos.
The minimum secondary school score for admission is 70%.

## Application fee
The application fee is 500 EGP, paid at the bank or online.

## Tuition fees
Tuition at DIT is 48,500 EGP per semester for all undergraduate programmes.

## Paying in installments
Tuition can be paid in 2 installments. Installments are also available for the summer semester.

## Withdrawal and refunds
A student who withdraws in the first week gets 90% of the tuition back, in week 2 or 3 they get 50% back. After week 3 there is no refund.

## Contact
The Admissions Office is in building B, room B014, open Saturday to Wednesday from 10:00 to 14:00.
