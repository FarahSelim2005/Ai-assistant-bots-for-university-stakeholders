# Delta Institute of Technology (DIT): Student Services

## Library
The DIT library is in building D2. It is open Saturday to Thursday from 9:00 to 21:00. Students borrow up to 3 books for 1 week.

## Clinic
The student clinic is on the first floor of building H. It is open from 8:30 to 16:00. Call extension 3105 for an appointment.

## Student ID cards
A lost student ID card is replaced at office A12. The replacement fee is 200 EGP.

## Buses
DIT buses come from Tanta and Shebin El Kom. A monthly bus pass costs 650 EGP.
