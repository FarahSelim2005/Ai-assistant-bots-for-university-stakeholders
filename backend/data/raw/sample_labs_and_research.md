## Artificial Intelligence Lab

The Artificial Intelligence Lab is located in Building B, Room B2.14. It is open
Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The lab has 30 workstations with
NVIDIA RTX GPUs for machine learning coursework and thesis projects. The lab
supervisor is Dr. Sample Supervisor, and questions can be sent to
ai.lab@example.edu. Students must book a GPU workstation at least 24 hours in
advance through the lab booking form. Staff members can reserve the whole lab for
workshops by emailing the lab supervisor one week in advance.

## Electronics and Embedded Systems Lab

The Electronics and Embedded Systems Lab is in Building A, Room A1.05. It is open
Sunday to Thursday from 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM. The lab provides oscilloscopes,
signal generators, soldering stations, Arduino and Raspberry Pi kits. Safety
training is mandatory before a first visit; the training session runs every
Monday at 10:00 AM. Components can be borrowed for up to 14 days with a valid
student ID. Contact: electronics.lab@example.edu.

## Biotechnology Research Lab

The Biotechnology Research Lab is located in Building C, Room C3.20. Access is
restricted to staff, graduate researchers and final-year students with a signed
supervisor approval form. Lab coats, safety goggles and closed shoes are required
at all times. Equipment includes a PCR machine, a centrifuge and a laminar flow
hood. Contact: biotech.lab@example.edu.

## Research Office and Funding

The Research Office supports staff and students who want to apply for research
funding. The internal research grant call opens every year in October, and
proposals are due by the end of November. Each grant provides up to 50,000 EGP
for equipment and consumables. Undergraduate students can join funded projects
as research assistants with the approval of the principal investigator.
The Research Office is in the Administration Building, Room 105, and can be
contacted at research.office@example.edu.

## Thesis and Publication Support

Bachelor thesis students can request access to research labs through their thesis
supervisor. The university library provides access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect
and the ACM Digital Library from campus and through the VPN. Staff who publish in
indexed journals can apply to the Research Office for reimbursement of article
processing charges.
