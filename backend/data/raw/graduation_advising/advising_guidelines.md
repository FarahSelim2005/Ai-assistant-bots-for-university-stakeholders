# GIU Advising Process: General Guidelines

Source: the presentation "Advising Process – General Guidelines", prepared by Heba El Mougy,
Academic Performance Department, German International University (GIU). Questions that this
guide does not answer go to the Academic Performance Department or the student's advisor.

## What advising means (definition of advising)

Advising means "Not Regular": a student on advising (on the advising list) is not a regular
student. Advising is a status and not a request: a student is placed on advising for one of the
reasons below; it is not something a student applies for.

Arabic: الادفايزنج (الإرشاد الأكاديمي) يعني إن الطالب مش ريجيولار (غير منتظم). الادفايزنج حالة مش طلب.

## Role of the advising committee members

The advising committee members (advisors) have three roles:
- Academic guidance: support students in choosing suitable courses.
- Compliance monitoring: ensure that schedules follow university policies.
- Graduation planning: support the formulation of comprehensive graduation plans.

## Reasons for assigning a student to the advising lists / advising system

A student is assigned to the advising lists (the advising system) for any of these reasons:
- Transfer: internal transfer or external transfer.
- Undeclared major.
- Failed courses:
  - 1-2 failed courses: the student failed or missed the 2nd makeup exam.
  - More than 2 failed courses: the student is not eligible for the 2nd makeup exam.
- ABS courses (drop attendance) from the previous Winter semester.
- Unattended courses.
- Not eligible for the B.Sc. (Bachelor thesis) or the Internship.
- Probation.

## Probation

A probation student is a student whose GPA is above 3.7 (GPA > 3.7). Probation is one of the
reasons for being on the advising list.

Probation students are not allowed to add any hours. Advisors should not issue a memo for
requests from probation students to add hours.

The guide lists "consequences of being under probation" as a topic but does not describe them.
For the consequences, contact the Academic Performance Department or your advisor.

Arabic: البروبيشن (الإنذار الأكاديمي) لما الجي بي إيه أعلى من 3.7. طالب البروبيشن ممنوع يزود أي ساعات.

## The advising process (steps)

The advising process has four steps, in this order:
1. Send appointment: the advisor sends the student an appointment.
2. Meeting with the student.
3. Confirmation of the schedule.
4. Process the schedule.

The advising timeline (start and end dates) will be added later; the guide does not give the
dates yet. The guide also mentions "no-show cases" (students who do not attend their advising
appointment) without describing what happens in that case.

## Order of courses (which courses to choose first)

When building an advising schedule, courses are chosen in this order:
- Failed courses are selected before unattended courses.
- Courses from older semesters are selected first (hierarchy of courses).
- Language courses (English and German) follow the English and German checklists.

## Adding courses or hours

- Adding courses or hours needs an add form.
- There is a maximum number of hours that can be added (maximum allowed hours to be added).
  The guide does not state the number.
- Probation students are not allowed to add any hours (no memo is issued for such requests).
- Each student's allowed hours are calculated individually: "Students' allowed hours don't have
  to be equal to their regular batch's semester hours."
- Minimum allowed load: 60% of the student's allowed hours.

## Schedule constraints

- The student keeps at least one day off besides Friday (one day off together with Friday).
- As far as possible, the student is not assigned to 5 consecutive slots.

## Electives selection and assignment

- Option 1: the student selected electives from the link → the assigned courses should appear on
  the advising system.
- Option 2: the student did not select from the link → the student should fill in an add form,
  even if the student does not need to add hours.

## Other rules for advising schedules

- Extra courses together with the B.Sc. (Bachelor thesis): need the extra hours form and must
  respect the maximum allowed load.
- Student confirmation: the student confirms the schedule by GIU email or signature.
- Financially blocked cases are handled separately (the guide gives no details).
- Order of tutorial and lab slots matters, especially for Computer Science (CS) students.

## Bachelor thesis / internship eligibility

Eligibility for the Bachelor thesis (B.Sc.) or the Internship depends on the failed or missing
courses before the 7th semester:

- Clear eligibility: total failed/missing courses before the 7th semester → 4 courses,
  distributed as 2+2.
- Exceptional eligibility: total failed/missing courses before the 7th semester → 4 courses,
  distributed as 0+3, 0+4, 1+3 or 3+1.
- Irregular B.Sc./Internship cases have to be reviewed with the supervisors of the advising
  committee, and a declaration is needed.

## Ticketing system and memo system

Ticketing system: the way to contact the IT department, for technical issues only, with no
exceptions.

Memo system: used for these requests:
- Deactivate courses, for a graduation case or a conflict. A graduation plan is needed if the
  reason is not a conflict.
- Increase the added hours to reach 4 (the reason should be clearly mentioned).
- Allow a clash, for graduation cases only.
- Un-process a schedule after the deadline.
- Other cases.

## Allowing a clash (schedule clash)

A clash means two courses in a student's schedule at the same time. A clash can only be allowed
through a memo, and only for graduation cases. Students who are not graduating cannot get a clash.

## Graduation plan

A graduation plan has a template. The guide covers when a graduation plan is needed and summer
plans, but gives no further details, except that deactivating courses for a reason other than a
conflict needs a graduation plan. For the template and summer plans, contact the Academic
Performance Department or your advisor.

## Topics this guide does not explain

The guide names these topics without details, so there is no official answer for them here:
the consequences of probation, the advising timeline dates, what happens in no-show cases, the
maximum number of hours that can be added, financially blocked cases, and the graduation plan
template and summer plans. Contact the Academic Performance Department or your advisor for them.
