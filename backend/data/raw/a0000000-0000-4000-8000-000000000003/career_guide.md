# GIU Internship and Career Guide

NOTE: Sections about GIU facts (career fairs, employers, the Internship Semester) are
based on GIU's official news pages and name their source. Sections marked "general
advice" are common career guidance, not official GIU rules. Check the official GIU
website or contact the university before relying on dates or details.

## GIU Career Fair 2025

Arabic names: معرض التوظيف، الكارير فير، معرض الشركات.
The GIU Career Fair 2025 was held on November 14, 2025 at the GIU campus in the New
Administrative Capital. It brought together 95 exhibitors from a wide range of
industries: FMCG, Engineering, Pharmaceuticals, IT and Software, Construction,
Marketing, Banking, Logistics, Real Estate and Research. It is a platform for GIU
students and alumni to connect with employers, explore job openings and gain direct
industry insights. The main purpose of the GIU Career Fair is to support the GIU
Internship Semester: students meet partners offering internship opportunities aligned
with their academic tracks. The date and exhibitor list of the next career fair were
not announced at the time of writing.
Source: GIU news, "The GIU Career Fair 2025", November 16, 2025.

## GIU Internship Semester

Arabic names: الانترنشيب، التدريب، تيرم التدريب، الإنترنشيب سيمستر.
At GIU, senior students complete a five-month internship as part of the GIU Internship
Semester. The GIU Career Fair is organized mainly to support it, so that students can
meet partner companies offering internships aligned with their academic tracks. For
the exact rules (which semester, eligibility, how the internship is approved and
graded), ask the GIU Career Center or your faculty.
Source: GIU news, "The GIU Career Fair 2025", November 16, 2025.

## How to introduce yourself to a recruiter (general advice)

You do not need a long speech. Prepare a 30-second introduction such as:
"Hi, I'm a GIU student studying [your major]. I have experience in [two or three
skills], and I recently worked on [a project or internship], where I [what you did].
I'm looking for internship opportunities where I can develop my skills. Could you tell
me what opportunities your company offers for students?"
Mention one concrete thing you did, not only a list of technologies.

## Questions to ask every company at the career fair (general advice)

Do not only ask "Are you hiring?". Ask:
- Do you offer internships for GIU students or fresh graduates?
- What skills do you look for in candidates?
- Do you have opportunities in [your field, for example backend development, marketing or finance]?
- Can I apply here, or should I apply through your careers website?
- When do applications open, and when should I expect feedback?
- Could I connect with you on LinkedIn to follow future opportunities?
Tip: if a recruiter says "apply online", ask for the exact job title or programme name.

## Career fair checklist (general advice)

Arabic names: تجهيز معرض التوظيف، تحضير الكارير فير.
Before the career fair:
- Print 5 to 10 copies of your CV.
- Save your CV as a PDF on your phone.
- Update your LinkedIn profile (and GitHub or portfolio if you have one).
- Prepare a 30-second self-introduction.
- Choose 5 to 8 priority companies and read about them.
- Prepare two projects you can explain confidently.
- Dress in smart casual or business attire.
- Bring a notebook, or use your phone to record recruiters' names.

## How to explain your experience (general advice)

Recruiters may ask about your internship or projects. For each one, prepare: the
problem, what you personally did, the tools or technologies you used, and the result.
Do not only list technologies; explain what you actually did with them. Use numbers
when you can (users, time saved, accuracy, team size).

## Questions recruiters might ask you (general advice)

Practise answering these out loud:
1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Why do you want to work for our company?
3. What did you learn in your last internship or project?
4. Tell me about a project you worked on and your role in it.
5. Which area interests you most, and why?
6. What are your strengths, and what are you currently improving?
7. When can you start an internship?

## After the career fair (general advice)

- Connect with the recruiters you met on LinkedIn the same day.
- Send a short thank-you message that mentions your conversation.
- Apply to the relevant vacancies within 24 to 48 hours.
- Follow up politely if you do not hear back after one or two weeks.

## Writing a strong CV (general advice)

Arabic names: السي في، الريزيوم، السيرة الذاتية.
- Keep it to one page as a student, in a clean layout without photos or graphics that
  applicant tracking systems (ATS) cannot read; save it as a PDF.
- Order: contact details (email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio), education (GIU,
  major, expected graduation, GPA if good), experience and internships, projects,
  skills, languages (including German), certificates and activities.
- Start each bullet with an action verb (built, designed, analysed, led) and show the
  result, ideally with a number: "Built a booking API in Spring Boot used by 200 users".
- Tailor the CV to each role: put the most relevant projects and skills first and use
  words from the job description.
- Remove spelling mistakes, unclear abbreviations, personal details such as age or ID
  number, and generic phrases like "hard-working" without evidence.

## Preparing for interviews (general advice)

Arabic names: الانترفيو، المقابلة الشخصية.
- Research the company: its products, values and the role.
- Answer behavioural questions with the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- For technical roles, revise your core subjects and be ready to explain every project
  on your CV in detail; practise problem-solving out loud.
- Prepare two or three questions to ask the interviewer.
- Be on time, dress appropriately, and send a thank-you message afterwards.

## How to apply for internships (general advice)

Arabic names: ازاي اقدم على تدريب، التقديم على الانترنشيب.
1. Decide which field and companies you want, and check if the internship can count for
   the GIU Internship Semester (ask the GIU Career Center or your faculty).
2. Prepare a tailored CV and a short cover letter or message.
3. Apply through company careers pages, LinkedIn, Egyptian job sites such as Wuzzuf and
   Forasna, and employers you met at the GIU Career Fair.
4. Track your applications (company, role, date, status) and follow up after one or two weeks.
5. Prepare for interviews and assessments while you wait.

## Skills and career paths: Informatics and Computer Science (general advice)

Skills: programming (Python, Java or C++), data structures and algorithms, databases and
SQL, web or mobile development, Git, cloud basics, networking and security basics, and
for AI tracks: machine learning, statistics and data analysis.
Career paths: software engineer (backend, frontend, mobile), data analyst or data
scientist, machine learning engineer, cybersecurity analyst or penetration tester,
cloud or DevOps engineer, QA engineer.

## Skills and career paths: Engineering (general advice)

Skills: CAD tools (SolidWorks, AutoCAD), MATLAB and Simulink, PLC programming and
automation, embedded systems (C, microcontrollers), electrical power systems, project
management basics, and technical report writing.
Career paths: automation and control engineer, robotics or mechatronics engineer,
automotive engineer, electrical power engineer, manufacturing or production engineer,
maintenance or project engineer.

## Skills and career paths: Business Administration (general advice)

Skills: Excel (advanced), financial analysis and accounting, digital marketing (social
media, SEO, analytics), supply chain basics, presentation and communication,
CRM tools, and data tools such as Power BI.
Career paths: financial analyst, auditor or accountant, banking roles, digital
marketing specialist, brand or product associate (FMCG), supply chain or logistics
coordinator, business analyst, consultant.

## Skills and career paths: Design and Architecture (general advice)

Skills: Adobe tools (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), 3D modelling (Rhino, 3ds Max,
Blender, SketchUp), AutoCAD and Revit for architecture, rendering, a strong portfolio,
and presenting design concepts.
Career paths: industrial or product designer, fashion or jewellery designer, interior
designer, architectural designer, visualisation or 3D artist, UI/UX designer.

## Skills and career paths: Biotechnology and Pharmaceutical Engineering (general advice)

Skills: laboratory techniques, GMP and quality standards, data analysis, regulatory
basics, scientific writing, and process engineering fundamentals.
Career paths: quality control or quality assurance, production or process engineer in
pharmaceutical companies, research and development, regulatory affairs, medical or
sales representative.

## Online career resources (general advice)

- LinkedIn: build a complete profile, follow companies, and connect with recruiters.
- Company careers pages: many employers (for example VOIS, Vodafone, PwC) publish
  internships and graduate programmes there.
- Egyptian job sites: Wuzzuf and Forasna.
- GitHub or an online portfolio to show your projects.
- GIU news pages and the GIU Jobs page for career fairs and opportunities.
