## Buildings and Room Numbering

The GIU campus has three buildings: Building A (Academic Building), Building M
(Main Administration Building) and Building S (Student Services Building).
Each building has five floors: the ground floor and floors 1, 2, 3 and 4.
A room number is the building letter followed by three digits, and the first
digit is the floor. Ground floor rooms are numbered 001 to 030, for example M005.
First floor rooms are numbered 101 to 120, second floor rooms 201 to 220, third
floor rooms 301 to 320 and fourth floor rooms 401 to 420. For example, room S214
is on the second floor of Building S, and room A312 is on the third floor of
Building A.

## Campus Layout and Directions

The campus road and car park run along the south side of the campus. Coming from
the car park, Building M (Main Administration Building) is the large building on
the right; it is the main GIU building and has the CIB ATM. Building S (Student
Services Building) is on the left, the building closest to the road, next to the
GIU swimming pool. Building A (Academic Building) is behind Building S, further
from the road, on the north-west side of the campus. The sports courts are north
of Building M, between Building A and Building M, and the Essen food court is east
of Building M. Every building has stairs and an elevator next to its entrance. To reach a room,
go to the building shown by the letter of the room number, then take the stairs
or the elevator to the floor shown by the first digit (0 means the ground floor).

## Building A (Academic Building)

Building A is the Academic Building. The ground floor (A001 to A030) has lecture
halls. The first floor has the Electronics and Embedded Systems Lab (A115). The
second floor has the Artificial Intelligence Lab (A210) and the Biotechnology
Research Lab (A218). The third and fourth floors (A301 to A320 and A401 to A420)
have the offices of the course supervisors.

## Course Supervisors' Offices in Building A

Course supervisors have their offices on the third and fourth floors of Building A:
- Introduction to Computer Science: Room A301
- Data Structures and Algorithms: Room A303
- Databases: Room A305
- Computer Networks: Room A307
- Software Engineering: Room A310
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning: Room A312
- Digital Electronics: Room A401
- Engineering Mathematics: Room A404
- Business Administration: Room A408
- Bachelor Thesis Coordination: Room A415

Supervisors hold office hours Sunday to Thursday from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
Students should email their course supervisor before visiting outside office hours.

## Building M (Main Administration Building)

Building M is the Main Administration Building. It has the Admission Office (M005)
and the Clinic (M012) on the ground floor, the Research Office (M105) and the
Academic Coordinator (M110) on the first floor, and the German Office (M201) on
the second floor.

## Admission Office

The Admission Office is in Building M, Room M005 (ground floor). It handles new
applications, admission tests, submission of school certificates and transfer
requests. It is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.
Contact: admission@example.edu.

## Academic Coordinator

The Academic Coordinator's office is in Building M, Room M110 (first floor). The
Academic Coordinator helps students with course registration, adding and dropping
courses, schedule clashes, study plans and academic warnings. Office hours are
Sunday to Thursday from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Contact: academic.coordinator@example.edu.

## German Office

The German Office is in Building M, Room M201 (second floor). It organizes German
language courses, the semester abroad in Germany, internships in Germany and
DAAD scholarship applications. It is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
Contact: german.office@example.edu.

## Clinic

The Clinic is in Building M, Room M012 (ground floor). A doctor and a nurse are
available Sunday to Thursday from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM for first aid, minor illnesses
and medical excuses. In an emergency, call the campus emergency line at extension
5555. Contact: clinic@example.edu.

## Building S (Student Services Building)

Building S is the Student Services Building. It has the Library (S010 to S020 on
the ground floor and S101 to S110 on the first floor), the Finance Office (S115)
on the first floor and the Student Affairs Office (S201) on the second floor.

## Library

The Library is in Building S. The main reading hall is on the ground floor
(S010 to S020) and the quiet study rooms are on the first floor (S101 to S110).
It is open Sunday to Thursday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Saturday from 10:00 AM
to 4:00 PM. Students can borrow up to 5 books for 14 days with their student ID.
Contact: library@example.edu.

## Finance Office

The Finance Office is in Building S, Room S115 (first floor). It handles tuition
fee payments, installment plans, refunds and payment receipts. Tuition fees can
be paid at the Finance Office by card or by bank transfer. It is open Sunday to
Thursday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Contact: finance@example.edu.

## Student Affairs Office

The Student Affairs Office is in Building S, Room S201 (second floor). It handles
student ID cards, enrollment certificates, student clubs and activities, and
student complaints. It is open Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Contact: student.affairs@example.edu.
